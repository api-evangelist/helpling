Autumn dresses our gardens in vivid shades of red, orange, and yellow, creating a picturesque landscape. But this colourful display leaves many homeowners wondering whether to rake up the fallen leaves or simply let them lie. Each choice has its own benefits and affects both the health of the garden and the surrounding environment.
